# Visual Design Fundamentals

## Composition
- Balance, proportion, alignment, symmetry, asymmetry, focal points

## Visual hierarchy
- Scale, contrast, emphasis, grouping, reading order

## Grid systems
- Columns, gutters, baseline grids, alignment and responsive grids

## Spacing
- Spacing scales, density, rhythm, negative space and section separation

## Typography
- Font selection, pairing, type scales, line length, readability and hierarchy

## Colour theory
- Hue, saturation, value, temperature, contrast and colour relationships

## Colour semantics
- Brand, neutral, action, success, warning, danger and informational colours

## Shape and geometry
- Form, silhouettes, corner treatments, geometry and visual consistency

## Depth
- Layering, elevation, shadows, blur, transparency and overlap

## Imagery
- Art direction, cropping, focal point, composition, authenticity and relevance

## Iconography
- Metaphors, legibility, consistency, recognizability and labeling

## Visual rhythm
- Repetition, variation, sequence, density and pacing

## Brand expression
- Identity, personality, tone, distinctiveness and consistency
