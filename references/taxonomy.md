## 1. Industry and Product Categories

### 02 · SaaS and business software

### 03 · E-commerce and retail

### 04 · Finance, banking and insurance
- Digital banking, fintech applications, payments and digital wallets, personal finance, budgeting and expense tracking, accounting, investment platforms, trading platforms, insurance portals, lending and financing, financial reporting, cryptocurrency and Web3 products

### 05 · Healthcare and wellness
- Hospital and clinic websites, patient portals, telemedicine, appointment booking, electronic health record interfaces, pharmacy and medication management, healthcare administration, fitness and workout apps, nutrition and wellness platforms, mental wellness products, medical equipment interfaces, health information portals

### 06 · Education and learning
- Learning management systems (LMS), online course platforms, educational websites, virtual classrooms, student and teacher portals, school and university systems, assessment and examination platforms, tutoring and coaching platforms, learning progress dashboards, research and academic platforms, educational games

### 07 · Media, publishing and entertainment
- Blogs and editorial websites, digital newspapers and magazines, video streaming, music and audio platforms, podcasts, movie and TV discovery platforms, gaming interfaces, creator platforms, membership and subscription content, digital libraries, news feeds and content aggregators, digital asset and media management

### 08 · Social and community
- Social networks, messaging applications, online forums, community platforms, professional networking, creator communities, dating applications, discussion and Q&A platforms, live streaming communities, user-generated content platforms, reviews and ratings platforms

### 09 · Travel, hospitality and mobility
- Travel booking, hotel and accommodation booking, airline and transport booking, itinerary planners, tourism websites, restaurant discovery and reservations, food delivery, ride-hailing, public transport applications, navigation and maps, car rental and vehicle sharing

### 10 · Real estate and property
- Property listings, rental marketplaces, real estate agency websites, property management, tenant and landlord portals, mortgage tools, virtual property tours, construction management, interior design and home planning

### 11 · Government, legal and nonprofit

### 12 · Operations, logistics and industrial

### 13 · Human resources and careers
- Job boards, recruitment platforms, applicant tracking systems, employee portals, payroll and benefits, performance management, learning and development, freelance marketplaces, career planning platforms

### B. Actions and controls

### C. Form inputs and selection

### D. Data display and collections

### E. Feedback and system status

### H. Data visualization

### I. Commerce and billing

### J. Layout and structural components

## 10. Component States and Interactive Behaviour

### Visual and interaction states
- Default, hover, focus, active/pressed, selected, unselected, expanded, collapsed, checked, unchecked, disabled, read-only, loading, valid, invalid, success, error, warning, pending, processing, completed, cancelled, partially completed, unsaved changes, stale/out-of-date, offline, permission denied

### Page and data states
- Initial, empty, loading, loaded, no results, partial results, failed request, timeout, network unavailable, session expired, authentication required, access denied, missing resource, deleted resource, conflicting update, rate limited, maintenance mode

### State-management questions
- What caused the state change? Is the change visibly communicated? Can the user undo or retry? Does the interface preserve entered information? Is the next action clear? Does the interface recover gracefully?

## 11. Master Classification: 15 Dimensions

| # | Dimension | Main question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Industry and product type | What are we designing? |
| 2 | Business and product goals | What must the product achieve? |
| 3 | Users and audience | Who will use it, and in what context? |
| 4 | UX research and discovery | What evidence informs the design? |
| 5 | Features and requirements | What must users be able to do? |
| 6 | Information architecture | How is the product organized? |
| 7 | User journeys and workflows | How do people complete their tasks? |
| 8 | UI components and functional elements | What interface elements are required? |
| 9 | Visual and aesthetic direction | How should the interface look and feel? |
| 10 | Layout and visual hierarchy | How is the information arranged? |
| 11 | Interaction and motion | How should the interface respond? |
| 12 | States and feedback | What happens during success, loading, and failure? |
| 13 | Accessibility and inclusive UX | Can different users operate and understand it? |
| 14 | Responsive design and engineering | Can it work reliably across devices and real conditions? |
| 15 | Validation and measurement | How will quality and success be evaluated? |
