## Conversion, Engagement and Growth UX

### Acquisition
Landing-page messaging, value proposition, CTAs, lead-generation forms, lead magnets, contact and demo booking, campaign landing pages, social proof, testimonials and case studies, trust signals

### Activation
Signup completion, onboarding completion, first-value experience, guided setup, sample content or demo data, feature discovery, checklists and progress indicators, time-to-value optimization

### Engagement and retention
Saved preferences, personalization, activity summaries, notifications, reminders, usage dashboards, progress tracking, re-engagement journeys, community features, loyalty programs

### Monetization
Pricing pages, plan comparison, free trials, freemium models, upgrade prompts, subscription management, add-ons, usage-based billing, renewal flows, cancellation surveys, win-back experiences

### Optimization
Conversion funnels, A/B testing, form optimization, checkout optimization, drop-off reduction, CTA testing, usability-based conversion improvement, retention and churn analysis

Conversion should be measured against meaningful outcomes and informed user choice, not merely clicks or forced engagement.

## Data Visualization and Analytical UX

### Visualization purposes
Comparison, trend analysis, distribution, composition, relationships, correlation, ranking, progress toward a goal, geographic analysis, process and flow analysis

### Analytical functionality
Date-range selection, timezone selection, filters and dimensions, sorting and grouping, drill-down, drill-through, cross-filtering, data aggregation, metric definitions, comparisons with previous periods, targets and benchmarks, forecasts and confidence intervals, annotations, export and sharing, saved reports, scheduled reports

### Data communication
Clear metric labels, units and scales, meaningful axis ranges, legends, tooltips, missing-data indicators, freshness timestamps, definitions of derived metrics, distinction between actual and estimated values, accessible colour choices, text alternatives or summaries for charts

## Trust, Privacy, Security and User Control

- Clear privacy policies, consent management, cookie preferences, permission requests, data collection explanations, secure sign-in flows, multi-factor authentication, session management, device and session visibility, account recovery, access control, role and permission transparency, payment security indicators, subscription transparency, cancellation and deletion controls, data export, audit history, sensitive-action confirmation, rate-limit and abuse feedback, scam and impersonation protections, undo and recovery options, error transparency, reliable persistence and autosave

### Ethical UX requirements
- No deceptive consent, no hidden costs, no misleading urgency, no deliberately obstructed cancellation, no preselected consent that violates applicable requirements, no confusing unsubscribe or account deletion processes, no manipulative notification patterns, clear distinctions between paid and free functionality

Trustworthy design makes the consequences of important decisions understandable before users commit.
