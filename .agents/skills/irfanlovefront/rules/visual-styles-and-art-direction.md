## B. Digital, geometric and experimental
Brutalism, neo-brutalism, experimental web design, maximalism, asymmetrical composition, geometric abstraction, kinetic typography, generative visuals, data-driven visual design, interactive art direction, anti-design

## C. Surfaces, depth and rendering
Flat design, material-inspired design, skeuomorphism, neumorphism, soft UI, glassmorphism, claymorphism, layered surfaces, tactile interfaces, realistic 3D interfaces, volumetric and dimensional design, gradient mesh design, atmospheric blur and translucency

## D. Premium, editorial and commercial
Luxury and high-end, editorial magazine, fashion editorial, premium corporate, refined and understated, boutique brand, modern commerce, art-directed product storytelling, gallery and portfolio, high-end agency presentation, professional enterprise, heritage and craftsmanship

## E. Playful, friendly and expressive

## F. Retro and cultural influences

## G. Natural, organic and human-centered
Organic shapes, earthy and natural palettes, botanical and nature-inspired, sustainable and eco-conscious, warm minimalism, humanist design, handmade and artisanal, soft organic gradients, natural textures, biophilic visual design

## H. Enterprise and information-heavy interfaces
Data-dense professional UI, operations-console aesthetic, technical dashboard, enterprise utility, developer-tool aesthetic, precision-oriented interface, instrument-panel inspired, high-information-density layout, mission-control aesthetic, functional utilitarian design

## I. Image, colour and visual treatments

## Related Terms

| Term | What it defines |
|---|---|
| Visual style | The overall appearance and art direction |
| Theme | A presentation mode, such as light or dark |
| Colour palette | The chosen colours and their relationships |
| Design system | Reusable rules, tokens, components, and standards |
| Layout | The spatial arrangement of content |
| Interaction pattern | How a user performs a task |
| Brand identity | The visual and verbal expression of a brand |
| Motion language | How animation and transitions behave |
| Product category | What type of product is being designed |
