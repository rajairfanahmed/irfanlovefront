# 01: Anti-Slop Directives & Banned Patterns
**Domain:** Creative Layout Engineering (Stage 2)  
**Philosophy:** Total eradication of AI template signatures. Zero em-dashes.

---

## 1. Production-Test Tells (Banned Outright)

These patterns are signatures the model defaults to when trying to "look designed". They are hard bans:

### Hero & Top-of-Page:
- **NO version labels in hero:** `V0.6`, `v2.0`, `BETA`, `INVITE-ONLY PREVIEW`, `EARLY ACCESS`, `ALPHA` are banned as default eyebrows.
- **NO "Brand · No. 01"-style sub-eyebrows:** Skip them.
- **Hero viewport cap:** Headline max 2 lines, subtext max 20 words and max 4 lines, top padding max `pt-24`, max 4 text elements total.
- **NO tiny tagline below CTAs:** "Works with GitHub, GitLab..." moves to a separate section.
- **NO trust micro-strip in hero:** Logo wall lives UNDER the hero, never inside it.

### Section Numbering & Micro-Labels:
- **NO section-number eyebrows:** `00 / INDEX`, `01 // ARCHITECTURAL DIRECTORY`, `001 Capabilities`, `06 how it works` are BANNED. Eyebrows should name the topic in plain language, not enumerate.
- **NO `01 / 4`-style pagination:** If the user can count, they do not need the label.
- **NO `Scroll · 001 Capabilities`-style scroll cues:** Banned.
- **EYEBROW RESTRAINT:** Maximum 1 eyebrow per 3 sections. Hero counts as 1.

### Separators & Dots:
- **Middle-dot (`·`) is rationed:** Maximum 1 per line in metadata strips.
- **NO decorative colored status dots:** Banned before nav links, badges, list rows. Acceptable only when conveying real semantic server or availability state.

### Em-Dashes & Typography Flourishes:
- **NO em-dash (`-` and `-`) anywhere on the page:** Complete ban. Use regular hyphen `-`.
- **NO `<br>`-broken and italicized headlines:** "for thirty<br>*years.*" type splits are banned.
- **NO vertical rotated text:** Agency-portfolio cliché.
- **NO crosshair / hairline grid lines as decoration:** Vertical and horizontal lines drawn just to make the page "feel designed" are banned.

### Fake Product Previews:
- **NO div-based fake product UI in hero:** Fake task lists, fake terminals, fake dashboards built from styled divs are banned. Use real images, generated images, or skip the preview.
- **NO fake version footers:** Inside fake screenshots ("v0.6.2-rc.1").

### Marketing-Copy Tells:
- **NO "Quietly in use at" / "Quietly trusted by":** Use natural language: "Trusted by", "Used at", "Customers include".
- **NO "From the field" / "Field notes" / "On our desks" style poetic labels:** Use plain functional labels.
- **NO weather / locale strips:** ("LIS 14:23 · 18°C") in headers/footers unless brief is about a place.
- **NO micro-meta-sentences under eyebrows:** Clutter.
- **NO generic step labels:** "Stage 1 / Stage 2 / Stage 3", "Step 1 / Step 2 / Step 3", "Phase 01 / Phase 02" in visible copy. Use the verb-noun directly ("Install", "Configure", "Ship").

### Image Overlays & Footers:
- **NO pills/labels/tags overlaid on images:** (`Plate · Brand`, `Field notes - journal`).
- **NO photo-credit captions as decoration:** (`Field study no. 12 · Ines Caetano`).
- **NO version footers on marketing pages:** (`v1.4.2`, `Build 0048`).
- **NO decoration text strip at hero bottom:** Patterns like `BRAND. MOTION. SPATIAL.` or `TYPE / FORM / MOTION` as a mono-caps strip across the bottom of the hero are an agency cliché and banned.
- **NO floating top-right sub-text in section headings:** Stack vertically or use 2-column header with visual on right.
- **NO scroll cues:** `Scroll`, `↓ scroll`, `Scroll to explore`.
