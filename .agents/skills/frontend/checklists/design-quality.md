# Design Quality Checklist

- [ ] Product category, audience, roles, and primary tasks are clear.
- [ ] Supplied documents and design references were inventoried and inspected.
- [ ] Reference observations are separated from interpretation.
- [ ] The design direction is distinct and supported by product evidence.
- [ ] Route-specific composition reflects content and task intent.
- [ ] Typography and color roles are deliberate and accessible.
- [ ] No generic repeated layout has been applied without a content reason.
- [ ] Realistic content and important states have been considered.
- [ ] Responsive composition is included from the beginning.
- [ ] Motion is purposeful and has a reduced-motion alternative.
- [ ] Public and private route behavior is understood.
- [ ] Backend contracts are respected or gaps are marked.
- [ ] Assumptions and unresolved decisions are visible.
