# Category Guide: Creative & Engineering Portfolio

## Visual & Structural DNA
- **Conversion Anchor:** Hiring inquiry, freelance project booking, proof of elite craft.
- **Hero Archetype:** H4 (Massive Typography Watermark & Editorial Reel) or H12 (Horizontal Filmstrip).
- **Key Components:**
  1. Live availability status pill ("Available for Q3/Q4 contracts").
  2. Case study cards with video preview on hover and project metrics.
  3. Interactive skills and tech-stack matrix.
  4. Career timeline / experience milestones.
  5. Direct email / contact trigger with copy-to-clipboard confirmation.
- **Typography:** Instrument Serif or Ogg display paired with Geist or Inter.
- **Anti-Patterns:** Boring unstyled resume bullets, generic template themes, uncredited stock work.
