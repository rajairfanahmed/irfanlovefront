# Category Guide: Agency & Production Studio

## Visual & Structural DNA
- **Conversion Anchor:** High-ticket client inquiry, RFPs, booking an architectural audit.
- **Hero Archetype:** H5 (Full-Bleed Video Reel Backdrop) or H4 (Massive Typography & Kinetic Reel).
- **Key Components:**
  1. Full-screen project showreel with ambient sound/play toggle.
  2. Large-scale case study grid with client impact metrics ("+340% Conversion").
  3. Interactive client roster with hover image preview.
  4. Industry recognition and award badges (Awwwards Site of the Day, FWA, Red Dot).
  5. Multi-step bespoke inquiry modal with budget selectors.
- **Typography:** Bold expressive display (Neue Montreal, Instrument Serif) with high contrast.
- **Anti-Patterns:** Looking like a generic corporate IT consulting firm, using corporate stock imagery.
