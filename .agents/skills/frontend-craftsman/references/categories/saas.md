# Category Guide: SaaS & Cloud Platforms

## Visual & Structural DNA
- **Conversion Anchor:** Free trial signup, interactive product demo, monthly/annual subscription.
- **Hero Archetype:** H1 (Split Viewport) or H2 (Centered Headline with Interactive UI Mockup).
- **Key Components:**
  1. Interactive pricing switcher (monthly vs annual with discount pill).
  2. Feature comparison matrix with sticky header.
  3. Logo ticker ("Trusted by 10,000+ teams").
  4. Tabbed deep-dive feature showcase with realistic screenshot states.
  5. FAQ accordion addressing common buying objections.
- **Typography:** Plus Jakarta Sans or Inter heading with Inter body.
- **Anti-Patterns:** Vague slogans, hiding pricing behind sales forms, generic blank placeholder screenshots.
