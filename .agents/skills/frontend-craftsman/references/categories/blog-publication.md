# Category Guide: Blog & Editorial Publication

## Visual & Structural DNA
- **Conversion Anchor:** Article reading time, newsletter subscription, bookmarking.
- **Hero Archetype:** H7 (Editorial Magazine Cover with Lead Feature Story).
- **Key Components:**
  1. Reading progress bar pinned to the top of the viewport.
  2. Sticky sidebar with Table of Contents tracking active heading scroll.
  3. Pull-quote blocks with distinct border and author attribution.
  4. Author byline with reading time estimate and publication date.
  5. In-article newsletter signup box and related articles grid.
- **Typography:** Classical serif body font (Merriweather, Source Serif, Instrument Serif) with generous line height (`1.75`).
- **Anti-Patterns:** Intrusive full-screen popups that block reading, tiny low-contrast body text.
