# Category Guide: E-Commerce & DTC Brands

## Visual & Structural DNA
- **Conversion Anchor:** Product purchase, collection exploration, add-to-cart rate.
- **Hero Archetype:** Full-bleed editorial seasonal collection banner with direct "Shop Now" trigger.
- **Key Components:**
  1. Product card grid with variant chips (color swatches, sizing pills) and quick-view modal.
  2. Slide-over cart drawer with free shipping progress bar.
  3. Star rating breakdown and customer review photo cards.
  4. Trust badge strip (secure checkout, 30-day money back, fast delivery).
  5. Cross-sell / recommended products carousel.
- **Typography:** Refined editorial sans (Satoshi / Neue Montreal) or luxury serif (Playfair / Caslon).
- **Anti-Patterns:** Low-contrast add-to-cart buttons, hidden shipping fees, broken image aspect ratios.
