# Design Benchmark: Vercel (Monochrome Minimalist)

## Visual Signature
- **Atmosphere:** Pure high-contrast monochrome (pure black `#000` or pure white `#FFF`).
- **Geometry:** Sharp 0px to 4px corners, strict rectangular grids, wireframe borders.
- **Typography:** Geist Sans and Geist Mono, strict hierarchy, tabular numerals for deploy times and metrics.
- **Accents:** Restrained single-color accents used solely for status indicators (green for deployed, red for error).
- **Surface Elevation:** Flat surfaces with subtle border dividers rather than heavy drop shadows.
