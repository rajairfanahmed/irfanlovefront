# Design Benchmark: Apple (Monumental Product Isolation)

## Visual Signature
- **Atmosphere:** Expansive white or deep black canvas with monumental breathing room.
- **Hero Focus:** Huge isolated hardware product photo rendered with realistic specular reflections.
- **Typography:** Giant display titles (`clamp(3rem, 7vw, 6rem)`) with tight tracking and lyrical subheadlines.
- **Scroll Choreography:** Pinned scroll scenes where the product smoothly rotates or unfolds as the user scrolls.
- **Call-to-Action:** Understated blue pill button accompanied by a "Learn more >" text link.
