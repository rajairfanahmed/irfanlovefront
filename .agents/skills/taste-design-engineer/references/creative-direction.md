## 1. Purpose & Problem Solved
AI models typically invent one standard design and present minor variations (e.g. changing blue to purple) as "different options". This reference mandates the formulation of three **materially distinct structural and aesthetic directions**, breaking the LLM's default template loop.

## 4. The 3 Materially Different Creative Directions Law

Merely tweaking colors, button radii, or background tints does NOT constitute a valid variation. The agent must formulate three fundamentally different creative hypotheses:

### Direction A: Editorial Broadsheet / High-Density Typographic Canvas
- **Visual Thesis:** Intellectual elegance, deliberate typographic hierarchy, and structural white space.
- **Spatial Geometry:** Asymmetric multi-column layout, rigid 1px physical hairline dividers, modular editorial margins.
- **Palette & Material:** Warm unbleached paper or rich bone canvas (`#F7F6F3`), deep carbon ink text (`#111111`), single muted editorial accent.
- **Best Suited For:** Thought leadership, architecture portfolios, premium writing platforms, luxury brands.

### Direction B: Machined Telemetry Console / Technical Architecture
- **Visual Thesis:** Precision engineering, instrumentation density, and operational authority.
- **Spatial Geometry:** Docked status bars, top telemetry indicators, split inspection viewports, live data monitors.
- **Palette & Material:** Dark titanium or deep slate canvas (`#08090A`), phosphor status accents (emerald, amber, cyan), crisp monospace telemetry labels.
- **Best Suited For:** Developer infrastructure, AI tooling, cyber-security, high-frequency trading platforms.

### Direction C: Kinetic Awwwards Canvas / Modernist Immersion
- **Visual Thesis:** Cinematic focal points, fluid spatial momentum, and expressive modernism.
- **Spatial Geometry:** Asymmetric 60/40 hero split, oversized display typography cutting across gutters, kinetic vertical snap cards.
- **Palette & Material:** OLED Vantablack (`#050505`), radial depth glow, frosted micro-glass borders, high-contrast action pop.
- **Best Suited For:** Design engineering studios, interactive showcases, venture-backed tech platforms.

---

## 5. Direction Evaluation & Selection Rationale

Every brief must compare the three directions against four objective criteria:
1. **Audience Psychology:** Does the direction command trust and engagement from the target demographic?
2. **Information Density:** Does it support the real volume of content without feeling barren or cluttered?
3. **Ergonomic Feasibility:** Can the spatial geometry collapse fluidly into a 320px mobile viewport?
4. **Brand Authenticity:** Does it express the client's actual mission rather than mimicking generic AI trends?

The agent selects the winning direction and documents the explicit trade-off rationale in the brief.

---

## 6. Anti-Template & Originality Enforcement

The agent must run this verification checklist before finalizing the visual thesis:
- **No Cliché Centered Heroes:** Is the hero deliberately structured with asymmetric balance rather than a generic centered headline and two identical buttons?
- **No Floating Generic Cards:** Are content surfaces anchored by physical borders, substrate changes, or layout grids rather than floating drop-shadow boxes?
- **No Rainbow Gradients:** Is the palette disciplined with one dominant substrate, one clear text ink, and a single intentional accent pop?
