## What Is New in Release 3.2.7

Release 3.2.7 is a milestone architectural upgrade to the entire irfanlovefront progressive skills suite (Skills 1 through 9). It permanently resolves the primary flaw of modern AI frontend generation: design sameness, template repetition, and cookie-cutter dark slate layouts.

### 1. Site Category Detection (12 Dedicated Archetypes)
Skill 1 (`/1-prompt-generator`) now classifies every website into one of 12 distinct categories during initial discovery:
- **SaaS & Cloud Platforms:** High-converting product hero, pricing tiers, feature comparison matrix.
- **Creative Portfolios & Design Studios:** Media-forward layouts, typography-heavy case studies, curated galleries.
- **E-Commerce & DTC Brands:** Product showcase, quick-buy triggers, customer social proof, cart flows.
- **Agency & Studio:** Bold portfolio reels, bespoke typography, service cards, client logos.
- **Editorial & Media:** Magazine-style reading rhythm, pull quotes, reading progress, article cards.
- **FinTech & Financial Services:** Security badges, live transaction ticker, data density, compliance footers.
- **Luxury & Fashion:** Generous negative space, editorial photography, refined serif type, subtle transitions.
- **Web3 & Crypto:** Interactive data cards, wallet triggers, protocol stats, tokenomics.
- **Healthcare & Wellness:** Calming color systems, clear benefit callouts, practitioner credibility.
- **Restaurant & Hospitality:** Menu archetypes, reservation triggers, ambient photography, location details.
- **Educational & Developer Documentation:** High scannability, interactive code blocks, structured navigation.
- **AI & Data Infrastructure:** Live pipeline diagrams, telemetry counters, command line previews.

### 2. 6-Axis Design Personality System
Rather than forcing every project into the same style, Skill 1 and Foundation lock 6 distinct personality vectors:
- **Color Energy:** Monochromatic / Dual-Tone / High-Contrast / Saturated Vibrant / Soft Pastel.
- **Visual Density:** Compact Information / Balanced Production / Generous Editorial Air.
- **Corner Radius & Surface:** Sharp 0px Brutalist / Crisp 4px-8px Modern / Soft 12px-20px Pill.
- **Typography Tone:** Technical Mono / Modern Neo-Grotesque / Classical Serif / Expressive Display.
- **Motion Feel:** Cinematic (Slow, Heavy) / Snappy (Quick, Spring) / Calm (Gentle Fade) / Reduced.
- **Layout Structure:** Strict Bento Grid / Asymmetric Editorial / Fluid Layered / Split Viewport.

### 3. Progressive Skills Upgrade (1 through 9)
- **`/1-prompt-generator`**: Generates dedicated `prompts/phase-1` through `phase-8` blueprints infused with category-specific requirements and locked personality vectors.
- **`/2-setup-foundation`**: Configures category-adaptive CSS tokens, typography pairings, and verbatim data structures.
- **`/3-build-components`**: Replaces the fixed 6-component formula with bespoke components tailored to the category.
- **`/4-cards-and-layout`**: Introduces 15 distinct Hero Archetypes (H1 through H15) and custom card architectures.
- **`/5-navbar-and-footer`**: 10 Navbar Archetypes, 6 Menu State Machines, 8 Contact Sections, and 7 Footer Archetypes.
- **`/6-build-pages`**: Category-specific section composition with alternating visual densities and zero repeated section layouts.
- **`/7-animate-website`**: 4 motion profiles matching the locked Motion Feel axis.
- **`/8-responsive-and-qa`**: Dynamic responsive and anti-bug QA checklist adapting to what was actually built across 375px, 768px, and 1440px viewports.
- **`/9-seo-and-aeo`**: Category-specific Schema.org entity mapping, semantic landmarks, and dual `llms.txt` + `llms-full.txt` optimization.

### 4. Daytona Open-Source Hygiene & Documentation
- Full integration of the Daytona 4,000-star launch documentation standard via `/github-readme-writer`.
- Zero em-dash (U+2014) and zero en-dash (U+2013) guarantee across all files, docs, and codebase.
