# PRD Review Prompt

## Step 3: The Complete PRD Review

Copy and paste this prompt into ChatGPT to get comprehensive feedback on your PRD:

```
Review this Product Requirements Document as an expert product manager. Focus on practical feedback that will improve the final result.

Check for:

**CRITICAL ISSUES** (must fix before development):
- Missing essential sections
- Unclear or contradictory requirements  
- Major technical or business risks
- Impossible timelines or scope

**IMPROVEMENT OPPORTUNITIES**:
- Features that could be simplified for MVP
- Missing user scenarios or edge cases
- Technical architecture concerns
- Business model or pricing issues

**POLISH SUGGESTIONS**:
- Clarity improvements
- Better organization
- Stronger value proposition
- More specific metrics

For each issue, tell me:
- Exactly where the problem is
- Why it matters
- What to do about it

Keep feedback actionable and prioritized. Focus on the biggest impact improvements first.

PRD TO REVIEW IS ATTACHED.
```

## How to Use This Review

1. **Paste your complete PRD** into the prompt above
2. **Run in ChatGPT** (GPT-4 recommended)  
3. **Address Critical Issues first** - these could block development
4. **Work through Improvements** - prioritize by impact
5. **Apply Polish Suggestions** - for final professional presentation

## What You'll Get

- **Prioritized feedback** - critical issues first
- **Specific locations** - exactly where to make changes
- **Actionable suggestions** - what to do, not just what's wrong
- **Business-focused** - practical improvements that matter

## Pro Tips

- **Copy sections separately** if your PRD is very long (ChatGPT has input limits)
- **Run the review twice** - once after major changes to catch new issues
- **Save the feedback** - use it as a checklist for future PRDs
- **Focus on Critical and Improvement sections** - don't get lost in polish details
